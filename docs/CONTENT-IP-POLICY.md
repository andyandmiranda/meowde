# Meowde Educational Content & IP Policy

## Purpose
Meowde may use course materials that the developer is authorized to access as references for general **learning objectives**. Materials provided for classroom use must not be assumed licensed for publication, redistribution, or incorporation in an app.

## Rules for new educational content
1. Record concepts and learning goals from authorized references, then write **new** questions, explanations, examples, distractors, datasets, filenames, and lab scenarios independently.
2. Never paste or closely paraphrase slides, worksheets, assessment prompts, answer keys, screenshots, diagrams, distinctive lab setups, or datasets without verified permission/license.
3. Standard Linux commands, documented syntax, factual definitions, and techniques may be taught; avoid copying any source-specific arrangement, narrative, illustrations, or substantial expressive presentation.
4. Do not bundle instructor PDFs, exercise ZIPs, course assets, logos, trademarks, or institution branding without explicit rights.
5. Attribute third-party materials when required, but attribution **does not replace permission**. For third-party packages and fonts, retain license notices and comply with license terms.
6. Do not claim endorsement, certification, affiliation, or approval by NVIDIA or the course provider. Describe course alignment factually and only when verified.
7. Keep a private provenance record for each content set: author, creation date, learning objective, any reference consulted, license/permission status, review decision. Avoid distributing private classroom material through this log.
8. Before publishing, conduct a source comparison for unusually similar expressions and distinctive scenarios, check asset and software licenses, and seek permission or professional legal review for unclear cases.
9. When rights are uncertain, **exclude the content** until cleared.

## Existing Linux pilot
- Ten multiple-choice questions in `v500-linux-lessons.js` were drafted as general Linux learning exercises, not copied from classroom PDFs.
- They require pedagogical and originality review before production publication.
- Generic commands and snippets should be independently verified in an isolated test environment.
- Any future E5/E6 alignment should rely on objectives, not importing their README files, answer keys, or specific lab setups.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Original question, solution, explanation and distractors
- [ ] Newly created scenario and test data
- [ ] No copied instructor assets, unique exercises or protected branding
- [ ] External libraries, images, fonts and audio checked for licenses
- [ ] No implicit affiliation claims
- [ ] Factual and technical accuracy validated
- [ ] Unclear rights escalated or material omitted

This document is a content-development policy, not a legal opinion or guarantee of non-infringement.
